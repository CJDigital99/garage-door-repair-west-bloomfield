A door that's come off its track is one of the most common — and most misunderstood — emergency calls we get. It usually happens after a minor collision, a broken cable letting one side of the door drop unevenly, or years of accumulated wear finally letting a roller slip free from its track.

Do Not Force an Off-Track Door

If your door is crooked, hanging at a visible angle, or stuck partway with visible gaps between the rollers and the track, stop trying to operate it immediately. Forcing it can bend the track further, damage door panels, strain the opener beyond its limits, or in the worst case, cause the door to fall. Call (947) 837-0211 and we'll get someone out quickly.

What Causes Doors to Come Off Track in West Bloomfield

Vehicle contact with the door while partially open. More common than it should be, especially on the curving, sometimes narrower streets around the lakes where driveway approach angles aren't always straightforward.

A broken cable or spring letting one side of the door drop unevenly.

Bent or damaged track sections, sometimes from age, sometimes from stored items leaning against the track.

Worn rollers that have lost their ability to stay properly seated, particularly on older homes throughout the township that haven't had regular maintenance.

Our Repair Process

We assess the full extent of the problem first. An off-track door is sometimes standalone and sometimes a symptom of an underlying cable, spring, or roller issue.

We realign the door on undamaged track sections.

We replace any bent or damaged track.

We swap worn rollers as needed.

We rebalance the full system and test smooth operation across the door's complete range of motion.

What Off-Track Repair Typically Costs

Cost varies depending on the underlying cause — a straightforward realignment is relatively affordable, while a repair also requiring track replacement or new rollers costs more.

Frequently Asked Off-Track Repair Questions

Is it safe to leave my door off-track overnight? We'd recommend not attempting to operate it, and we can often prioritize an off-track call given the safety implications.

Will insurance cover an off-track repair from a vehicle collision? This depends on your policy — we're happy to provide documentation for a claim.

Can an off-track door fall completely? In serious cases, yes, which is why we recommend against continued operation until it's assessed.
