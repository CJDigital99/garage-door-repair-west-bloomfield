If you can see daylight around the edges of your closed garage door, feel a draft, or notice water pooling inside after rain or snowmelt, your weather seal has likely worn out or cracked. This matters throughout West Bloomfield, and especially for homes near the township's lakes, where ambient moisture puts extra strain on seals year-round, not just in winter.

Why This Matters More in a Lake Township

Michigan winters put real strain on weather seals — the rubber compound hardens and becomes brittle in extreme cold. West Bloomfield's lake geography adds a second factor: homes near Cass Lake, Pine Lake, Orchard Lake, and Walnut Lake deal with consistently higher ambient humidity than homes further inland, which can accelerate wear on seals and increase the risk of water intrusion during heavy rain or snowmelt.

What a Failing Seal Actually Costs You

Beyond discomfort, a failed seal has real ongoing costs — a harder-working heating system if your garage is attached to living space, potential damage to stored items from water intrusion, and conditions more inviting to pests looking for shelter, especially as Michigan temperatures drop in fall.

What We Replace

Bottom seal, the most common failure point.

Side and top weather stripping.

Threshold seals, for garages with finished space behind them, providing extra protection against water intrusion — particularly valuable for homes closer to West Bloomfield's lakes.

Our Replacement Process

We inspect all seal points.

We measure and source the correct seal profile for your door.

We install and test, confirming a proper seal across the full width and height of the door.
