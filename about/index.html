West Bloomfield Garage Door Experts is a locally run garage door repair company built around a simple idea: show up fast, quote honestly, and fix it right the first time. We're not a national call center dispatching whoever's available from a rotating subcontractor network, we're technicians who work West Bloomfield's neighborhoods every day and understand what makes this township different from a typical Oakland County suburb: the lakes, the concentration of custom and higher-end homes, and the genuine variety of housing stock from established mid-century properties to newer lakefront construction.

Why We Started This Company

Mark Delaney has been repairing and installing garage doors across Oakland County for 16 years, and built this company specifically to serve West Bloomfield with the kind of honest, upfront service that's sometimes harder to find from bigger regional outfits juggling a wide territory. After years of working for companies that prioritized upselling over honest diagnosis, we wanted to build something different, a local garage door company that West Bloomfield homeowners could trust to tell them the truth about what actually needs fixing, whether that's a routine sensor realignment or a full custom door installation on a lakefront estate.

What Sets Us Apart in West Bloomfield

Genuine understanding of the township's lake geography. We know that homes near Cass Lake, Pine Lake, Orchard Lake, and Walnut Lake see accelerated hardware corrosion from ambient humidity, and we factor that into our maintenance recommendations rather than applying a one-size-fits-all schedule regardless of location.

Real experience with custom and higher-end doors. West Bloomfield's concentration of larger, architecturally distinctive homes means custom and carriage-house door work is a regular part of what we do, not an unusual special-order request we're unprepared for.

Honest recommendations, even when they cost us a bigger job. If a repair genuinely makes more sense than a replacement, that's what we'll recommend, even on a job where a full custom door installation would be the larger sale.

Understanding of Michigan winters' effect on garage door systems. A significant share of our business is directly shaped by the freeze-thaw cycle's effect on springs, cables, and openers, and we've built our seasonal approach, from stocking common parts ahead of winter to proactively recommending fall tune-ups, around that reality.

Licensed, Insured, and Accountable

Every technician on our team is background-checked, trained specifically on garage door systems, and fully insured. We carry general liability coverage on every job, and we stand behind our work with a 2-year warranty on parts and labor.

Our Approach to Every Call

Whether it's a ten-minute sensor realignment or a full custom carriage-door installation on a lakefront property, every job gets the same core approach: a genuine diagnosis of what's actually wrong, a clear explanation in plain language, an upfront price before any work begins, and a level of care that respects your home, your time, and, given the significant value of many homes throughout this community, the trust you're placing in us.

Serving West Bloomfield and Beyond

We work throughout West Bloomfield Township, near Cass Lake, Pine Lake, Orchard Lake, Walnut Lake, and the areas further inland along Farmington Road and Pontiac Trail, as well as Orchard Lake Village, Sylvan Lake, Keego Harbor, Bloomfield Hills, Farmington Hills, Commerce Township, and Walled Lake.
