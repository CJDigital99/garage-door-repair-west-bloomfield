The Michigan Weather Factor

Michigan winters are genuinely hard on garage door hardware, and West Bloomfield's lake-heavy geography adds a second layer most other Oakland County suburbs don't deal with to the same degree. Cold weather makes spring steel more brittle, which is why springs that have quietly worn out over months of use tend to finally snap during the coldest stretches of a Michigan winter. But the lakes add humidity and moisture exposure that accelerates corrosion on cables, hinges, and track hardware, especially on homes closer to Cass Lake, Pine Lake, Orchard Lake, and Walnut Lake. We see this pattern constantly, and it shapes how we approach maintenance recommendations for lakefront and near-lake properties specifically.
